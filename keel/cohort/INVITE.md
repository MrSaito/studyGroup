## Email (longer)

**Subject:** Would you test Keel with me for 8 weeks?

Hi —

I built a small app for goals that take months, and I'm looking for 20–30 people to use it for 8 weeks before I decide what it becomes.

**What it is.** Keel takes a plan (a bundled 36-week AI engineer roadmap, or anything you paste in Markdown) and shows you exactly one unit each day: what to learn in 15 minutes, what to do in 45, and the one trap people fall into that day. Miss a day and the sequence slides forward; there is no backlog, no streak to protect, and nothing turns red. It works fully offline and syncs if you sign in.

**What I'm asking.** Install it (two minutes, guide below), pick a plan, and use it on the days you'd planned to study anyway. When something feels wrong — a screen, a word, a nudge at a bad time — reply with one line. I'll read every one.

**What I'm measuring.** Two numbers only: whether people complete at least three units a week, and whether people who fall off for a few days come back. Nothing is sold, nothing is shared, and you can delete everything from Settings at any time.

**Install:** https://keel-hshahfahad58-2498s-projects.vercel.app/ — open in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), then *Add to Home screen*. Step-by-step with screenshots: https://claude.ai/artifact/1vtcS5GpPzPJfK6TCgN212

Thank you — Saito
